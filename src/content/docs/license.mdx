[PolyForm Noncommercial License 1.0.0](https://polyformproject.org/licenses/noncommercial/1.0.0).

## What you may do

You are free to **use, self-host, and modify** HealthLog for any **noncommercial**
purpose. Running it on your own NAS, homelab, or VPS for personal or household
use, sharing changes with the community, and learning from the source are all
squarely within the licence.

The PolyForm Noncommercial licence grants permission to use the software for any
purpose **other than a commercial one**. "Noncommercial" means not primarily
intended for or directed toward commercial advantage or monetary compensation.

## What needs a separate agreement

Commercial use requires a separate arrangement. That includes, for example,
offering HealthLog (or a modified version) as a paid or ad-supported hosted
service, or using it inside a commercial product or business operation. If that
describes your use, please reach out via [healthlog.dev](https://healthlog.dev)
or open an issue on the
[GitHub repository](https://github.com/MBombeck/HealthLog) to discuss terms.

## Licence history (AGPL ≤ v1.15.18)

HealthLog was previously published under the **GNU AGPL-3.0**. The licence
changed to PolyForm Noncommercial 1.0.0 as of **v1.15.19**.

Releases **up to and including v1.15.18** were published under AGPL-3.0 and
**remain available under that licence** — the change is not retroactive. If you
have a specific need for an AGPL-licensed build, those tagged releases continue to
carry their original terms. Everything from v1.15.19 onward is PolyForm
Noncommercial.

## Why the change

A noncommercial licence keeps HealthLog free and open for the self-hosting
community it was built for, while reserving commercial exploitation for a separate
agreement. The project stays source-available and freely modifiable for the people
who run it themselves; what changes is that turning it into a commercial offering
now requires a conversation.

## Commercial enquiries

For commercial licensing, partnership, or any use that falls outside the
noncommercial grant, contact the maintainers through
[healthlog.dev](https://healthlog.dev) or the GitHub issue tracker. Include a
short description of the intended use so the terms can be scoped appropriately.
