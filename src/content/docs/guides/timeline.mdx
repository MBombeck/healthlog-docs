The timeline puts the years side by side: conditions, allergies, medications,
vaccinations, doctor visits and procedures, labs and documents on one time
axis, with up to three of your values running underneath. It arrived in
v1.42.0 as a module of its own.

## Switch it on

The timeline is **off by default**. Turn it on under **Settings, Modules**,
**Timeline**. An operator can make it unavailable for the whole instance like
any other module.

Switching it on first shows what your timeline already carries. Each lane is
rated as carrying, thin or empty, with one link per gap that takes you to the
place where the missing entries go, for example a medication without a start
date. A short verdict says whether the timeline gives an overview yet or will
mostly show your values for now. You can open the timeline straight away or
come back later.

While a gap is open, the same overview sits as a card on the timeline that you
can hide. It stays reachable under **Layers**, **Data coverage**.

Empty lanes stay hidden until something is in them, and a module you switched
off never appears.

## Reading it

On a window 768 px and wider the lanes are drawn on one time axis with a
year, month or week grid. Zoom between **All**, **12 months** and **3 months**.
An open period ends in an arrow, a dashed lead-in marks a start that is not
known, and dose changes and pauses sit on the medication they belong to. A
line marks today.

Below the lanes, a menu lets you add up to three value lines, drawn as monthly
means. The legend says it once: things stacked at the same time happened at the
same time, which is not cause and effect.

Select a month and the bar under the chart lists its entries and the month's
means, with a link that opens a day from it in the
[day view](/guides/day-view/).

From the keyboard the chart is one tab stop. The arrow keys move the selection
by a day, a month or a year depending on the zoom, Home and End jump to the
ends, and Enter opens the day. A screen reader gets a table of every entry.

On a phone the timeline is a chronicle instead: newest first, grouped by month
or year, with an **Ongoing** block for what has no meaningful start, a rail
beside the rows an episode or a course spans, and empty stretches named as
such.

## Life events

A life event is an anchor for the years, not a reading: a move, a birth, a new
job, a loss. Add one from the timeline, from the add menu on the dashboard or
from the capture picker. It has:

- a title of up to 120 characters,
- a kind: family, home, work, loss or other,
- a date known to the day, the month or only the year,
- an optional end, when it was a period,
- an optional note.

Title and note are stored encrypted. Deleting an event asks first.

In this release life events are yours alone. Nobody you share your record
with sees them, at any level, and a guardian cannot add or change them. They
are never sent to the Coach, to a connected MCP client or to any other model;
the Coach's [day reads](/reference/mcp/#read-tools) leave them out as well.
Whether they should become shareable is a separate question for a later
release.

## Sharing and the timeline

On a record someone shared with you, the timeline shows only the lanes the
share covers, and the life lane never.
