Two pieces of context a clinician always asks for do not fit a chart: what you
react to, and what runs in your family. HealthLog keeps both as **structured
records** so they travel with the rest of your data into the
doctor-report PDF and the FHIR export — rather than living in a note you have to
remember to mention.

## Allergies

An allergy record is structured rather than free-text:

- **Substance** — what you react to.
- **Reaction** — what happens.
- **Severity** — how serious it is.

To add one, open the records section and enter each field; editing an existing
record updates it in place. Keeping the three fields separate is what lets the
record map cleanly into the FHIR export below.

## Family history

Family history is recorded **condition-by-relative** — a condition paired with
the relative it applies to — so the picture is structured rather than a
paragraph. Enter and edit entries the same way as allergies.

## What is encrypted

The free-text parts of both records — the reaction description and any notes —
are stored under the same AES-256-GCM scheme described in
[encryption at rest](/explanation/encryption-at-rest/). The substance and
condition labels are deliberately queryable plaintext, following the same
precedent as medication names, so lists and the FHIR mapping can filter on
them; the structured enums (category, type, severity, status, relationship)
are plaintext too.

## What the AI sees

Structured allergies and family history feed the **Coach's self-context by
default**: the Allergies and Family history sections are on the health-profile
AI section list, and you can exclude either one under the health-profile AI
settings. They remain **off the MCP connector** — no MCP read tool resolves
them. The per-section control matches the exposure model in the
[privacy architecture](/explanation/privacy-architecture/).

:::note[Immunizations have their own log]
Vaccinations are tracked in their own immunization log rather than here. The
module ships enabled, and the vaccination history is part of the health
background section when you share your record.
:::
