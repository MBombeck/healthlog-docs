Two pieces of context a clinician always asks for do not fit a chart: what you
react to, and what runs in your family. HealthLog keeps both as **structured,
encrypted records** so they travel with the rest of your data into the
doctor-report PDF and the FHIR export — rather than living in a note you have to
remember to mention.

## Allergies

An allergy record is structured rather than free-text:

- **Substance** — what you react to.
- **Reaction** — what happens.
- **Severity** — how serious it is.

To add one, open the records section and enter each field; editing an existing
record updates it in place. Keeping the three fields separate is what lets the
record map cleanly into the FHIR export below.

## Family history

Family history is recorded **condition-by-relative** — a condition paired with
the relative it applies to — so the picture is structured rather than a
paragraph. Enter and edit entries the same way as allergies.

## Encrypted at rest

Both records are **encrypted at rest**, like the rest of your sensitive data.
They are stored under the same AES-256-GCM scheme described in
[encryption at rest](/explanation/encryption-at-rest/) — there is no plaintext
copy.

## Kept off the AI surface

These structured records are **not exposed to the Coach or MCP**. They are not on
the AI exposure allowlist and do not resolve through any read tool — they are
yours to surface to a clinician, not data streamed to a model. This matches the
deliberate exclusions in the [privacy architecture](/explanation/privacy-architecture/).

:::note[Immunizations are out of scope]
HealthLog does not keep immunization records. That is a deliberate scope
decision, not an oversight — there is no vaccination log to fill in.
:::
