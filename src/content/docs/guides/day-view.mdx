Since v1.42.0 every date in the app can open its day. The day is a layer over
the page you are on, so you look at a Tuesday in March and close it again
without losing your place.

## Where a day opens from

- **A chart drawn in days.** Click a point or a bar. On a touch screen the
  first tap shows the value, and the tooltip offers the day; that second step
  keeps a scroll from opening a day by accident. Under the axis a quiet row of
  dots marks the days that hold entries, and a ring marks a notable one.
- **A chart's data table**, where each date is a link.
- **Lists.** Measurements, mood entries, intakes, lab readings, symptoms and
  illness days, documents and workouts open their day with one tap, on a phone
  too.
- **Calendars and heatmaps**, such as the mood calendar and the adherence
  heatmap.
- **A Coach answer.** When the Coach looked at particular days to answer, those
  days appear as chips under the answer.

Some places deliberately do not open a day: the today area of the dashboard,
the small charts inside tiles, the cycle calendar, text a model wrote, future
dates, a clinician share link and the doctor-report PDF.

The address carries the open day as `?day=YYYY-MM-DD`, so a bookmark or a link
reopens it. A malformed or future date is simply dropped from the address.

## How it opens

On a wide window (1280 px and up) the day docks beside the page, the way the
Coach's conversations panel does, and the page reflows next to it. The control
at its top collapses the day to a narrow edge on the right instead of closing
it. The edge holds the date and a button that opens the last day again, on
this page or any other. The browser remembers that last day for your account
and record and forgets it when you sign out. Below that
it opens as a sheet from the right. On a phone it is a bottom sheet that starts
at a little over half the height; drag its handle up for the full height. On
the Coach page the day is always a sheet, so there is never more than one
docked panel.

Back or Escape closes the day. The arrows in its header step to the previous
or next day without filling your browser history, and Alt with the left or
right arrow key does the same from the keyboard.

## What the day shows

- **The value you opened it from**, with the reading before it for comparison.
- **What ran through the day**: medications and courses with their dose and
  day number, pauses, an illness with its day number, a cycle phase. Only
  records with a start date of their own appear here. Profile details such as
  smoking, alcohol or a work schedule describe you rather than the day and are
  not listed, and a medication saved without a start date shows no day count.
- **The readings**, each placed on a short line against your own usual range
  over the 30 days before. Eight are shown first; the rest sit behind **All
  values**. Several readings of one type on the day show as their mean, marked
  **Average of n readings**.
- **What happened**, in the order of the clock: intakes, dose changes,
  symptoms, lab results, visits, vaccinations, check-ups, documents, mood
  entries, workouts. Each row links to its record.
- **Notable observations**, decided by fixed rules rather than a model: the
  highest or lowest daily value for at least three months, or the first
  reading of a kind.

The footer offers **Add for this day**, which opens the capture forms on that
date, and the timeline when the [timeline module](/guides/timeline/) is on.
**Ask the Coach about this day** starts a question about it.

Days are cut in your own time zone. A reading at 23:30 belongs to the day you
lived it, also when you travelled across time zones.

## On a record someone shared with you

You see the sections the share covers. Anything else is named as not shared
rather than shown as empty, and is not read at all. Weather and air quality
and life events stay with the record's owner.

## Before the next visit

A planned visit shows what happened since the last one: every change the
record holds (dose changes, medications and courses that started or ended,
pauses) and every notable reading, including a regular reading that stopped
for two weeks or more. Each date opens its day, and the block offers a
[doctor report](/guides/checkups-and-practitioners/) for exactly that stretch.
