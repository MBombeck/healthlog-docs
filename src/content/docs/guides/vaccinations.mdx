You have a vaccination pass and want the doses in HealthLog, with the series
position worked out for you and a booster reminder you control. The
vaccinations module is on by default; switch it off under Settings, Modules
if you do not want the surface. Switching it off hides the page and the
navigation entry, but a booster reminder you already confirmed keeps firing,
because it is an ordinary preventive-care reminder.

The module records what happened. It never computes what you are due from
your age, sex or history; that judgement stays with you and your clinician.

## Record a dose

Open `/vaccinations` and add a dose. One row is one dose. Fields:

- **Date.** Day only, no time; a future date is refused.
- **Vaccine.** Pick an antigen or combination from the catalog, or type the
  name printed in your pass (a trade name is fine). One of the two is
  required.
- **Dose number** (1 to 20) and, optionally, the **series length** if your
  schedule differs from the catalog's typical count.
- **Lot number**, kept as plain text so it can be searched.
- **Site**: left or right arm, left or right thigh, oral, nasal, other.
- **Practitioner** and **visit**, both optional links to your own records
  (see [Checkups and practitioners](/guides/checkups-and-practitioners/)).
- **Note**, the one free-text field, encrypted at rest. Reactions belong here.

You can also attach scanned pass pages from the
[document vault](/guides/document-vault/). **Link a document** offers the whole
vault, with documents dated within seven days of the dose on top and the rest
by month, searchable. One document can be linked to every dose it covers, and
since v1.39.0 you can do that from the document's own sheet in the vault too,
which is quicker for a childhood record that lists a dozen doses. See
[Linking a document to visits and vaccinations](/guides/document-vault/#linking-a-document-to-visits-and-vaccinations).
Deleting a dose is soft and can be undone.

Since v1.39.1 an opened dose shows the documents linked to it, and saving a
change to the dose, such as a lot number, leaves its links in place unless you
change them yourself. Before, a dose opened from the list showed no linked
documents, and saving any change removed every one of them. If a dose lost its
pages that way, link them again from the dose or from the document.

## The catalog and series counting

The catalog is a fixed list of 35 entries over 26 antigens, each with its
components, a typical series length, a booster interval in months, a category
(standard, standard from 60, childhood, indication, travel) and a source
citation. It carries no trade names. A combination vaccine counts into each of
its components: one Tdap dose is a dose of tetanus, diphtheria and pertussis
at their own positions.

The "dose 3 of 3" label is computed on the server over all your live doses per
antigen. The number written in your pass wins over the chronological index;
the total is your own override, else the catalog's typical count, else
nothing. Once a series is complete, further doses are shown as boosters.

## Booster reminders

After a dose, the app can prefill a booster reminder from the catalog's
interval. Nothing is written until you confirm or edit it. The confirmed
reminder is a normal preventive-care reminder that appears on `/checkups`,
in the daily digest and through the MCP preventive-care tool, on a rolling
interval capped at ten years, notifying at 09:00 unless you change it. Once
due, it stays due after its notification until you log the dose, mark it done,
skip it or snooze it (see
[When a reminder is due](/guides/checkups-and-practitioners/#when-a-reminder-is-due)). There
is at most one booster reminder per antigen; confirming again re-anchors it
rather than duplicating it. Logging a later dose of any matching component
clears the reminder.

Since v1.39.0 the vaccination page shows a planned booster next to that
vaccine's heading: **Next booster** with the date, **Booster due** once it is
within 30 days, and **Booster overdue since** once the date has passed. The
last two are highlighted. The app does not work out booster dates on its own;
a vaccine without a confirmed booster reminder shows nothing there. Someone you
shared your record with sees the booster state only when their access reaches
your readings, where the reminders live.

## Where the record goes

- **Doctor report.** The immunisation section is part of the standard
  template and lists your lifetime doses with series positions. The
  encrypted note is never read into the report.
- **Backup and restore.** Doses and their document links round-trip.
- **FHIR export.** Not included; there is no Immunization resource in the
  bundle yet.

## API

`GET` and `POST /api/vaccinations`, `GET`, `PATCH` and `DELETE
/api/vaccinations/{id}`, `POST /api/vaccinations/{id}/restore`, `POST
/api/vaccinations/{id}/booster` to mint or re-anchor the reminder, `POST` and
`DELETE /api/vaccinations/{id}/links` for documents, and `GET
/api/vaccinations/suggest?anchor=<date>`. The routes stay reachable with the
module off so restore and import keep working.

Since v1.39.0 the list from `GET /api/vaccinations` carries `renewals`, one
entry per antigen with a confirmed booster: `dueAt`, `daysUntil` on the
person's own calendar, and `state` (`current`, `dueSoon` within 30 days, or
`overdue`). Render from `state` rather than working it out again. It is `null`
for a grant that does not reach the readings the reminders live in. From the
document end, `PATCH /api/documents/inbound/{id}` takes `vaccinationIds` as the
full set of linked doses (up to 100), and the document detail returns them as
`vaccinationLinks`.

Under a shared-access grant the dose is a Health background write, but the
booster it suggests is a Readings row, so the mint answers `403` with
`meta.errorCode: vaccination.booster-out-of-scope` for a grant that opens only
Health background, and nothing is written. Since v1.38.16 the pass offers add,
edit and delete only to a grant whose level and sections reach them.
