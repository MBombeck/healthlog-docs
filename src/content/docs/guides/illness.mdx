You are coming down with something, or managing a condition that flares, and
want a record of it next to your vitals. The illness module keeps episodes
with per-day logs, freezes streaks while you are unwell, and afterwards
shows a retrospective of how your body tracked against how you felt. It is
on by default and can be switched off under Settings, Modules; with the
module off, every illness route refuses, including for the native app.

The retrospective looks backwards only. It never predicts, and a red flag
only ever escalates to "consider seeking care"; it never reassures.

## Open an episode

On `/illness`, start an episode with:

- **Label**, your own words ("cold", "hay fever").
- **Type**: infection, allergy, injury, mental health, autoimmune, chronic,
  or other.
- **Body site** and **Side**, optional on every type: where on the body, in
  your own words ("knee", "lower back"), and left, right, both sides or not
  stated. The site is encrypted at rest.
- **Lifecycle**: acute (default), chronic ongoing, recurring, or flare. A
  flare can be threaded under a parent condition.
- **Onset**; defaults to now.
- **Note**, encrypted at rest.

A chronic ongoing episode has no end date by design. Any other episode is
closed with **Mark recovered**, or by setting the resolved date when editing.
Deletion is soft with undo.

## Body site

While you type a body site, the field suggests the sites already in your
record, from visits and from other conditions, most used first, so the left
knee is written the same way every time. The suggestions narrow as you type
and never restrict: anything up to 200 characters is accepted.

An episode with a body site shows it in its header, with the side when one is
set ("Knee · Left"). Tapping it opens the
[Body sites](/guides/checkups-and-practitioners/#body-sites) tab under
`/checkups` on that site and side, with the visits and other conditions filed
there.

To remove a site, clear the field and set the side to **Not stated** when
editing. Editing a condition in the iPhone app keeps a site added on the web,
because the app does not send the field.

## Log a day

Each episode takes one log per calendar day:

- **Functional impact** on a four-step slider, from fully functional to
  bedbound.
- **Fever** in degrees Celsius, optional; stored as a plain number so the
  fever curve can be drawn.
- **Symptoms** from a short catalog (runny nose, stuffy nose, sneezing, sore
  throat, cough, headache, body aches, fatigue), each with a severity from 1
  to 3.
- **Note**, encrypted.

## While you are unwell

An active episode puts the account into rest mode. The health score keeps
its value with a "you were unwell" note instead of dropping, achievement
streaks freeze rather than break, and cadence nudges pause. Rest mode carries
only the label, type and onset; the note is never read.

Sustained low blood oxygen or sustained fever raise a red flag notification.
The thresholds are the app's fixed clinical floors and are not configurable.

## The retrospective

Once an episode has at least four days of data, the app looks at your vitals
(resting heart rate, temperature, respiratory rate, blood pressure, HRV,
recovery, blood oxygen) against a 30-day baseline that ends before the
episode, and reports three things:

1. **Pre-onset anomalies**: which vitals had already moved in the seven days
   before onset, in robust standard deviations from your own baseline.
2. **Nadir**: how far each vital strayed during the episode.
3. **Recovery gap**: the difference in days between the day your vitals were
   back in band for three consecutive days and the day you marked yourself
   better. A positive gap means the body lagged the feeling.

A vital with fewer than seven baseline days is left out rather than guessed.
Across episodes, the insights view tallies how often you were ill by month
and type and shows a typical recovery gap once at least three episodes with
enough coverage exist and the median gap is at least two days; otherwise it
is withheld.

The same read is available to the Coach and through the MCP
`get_illness_recovery` tool.

## Settings and where it goes

Settings, Appearance, Illness holds the card-or-list view and the manual
order; both are stored in the browser. Episodes are a leaf of the standard
doctor-report template: the PDF lists those that overlap the report period in
the **Conditions & illnesses** table. When at least one of them has a body
site, the table gains a **Body site** column after the condition; a report
without any keeps its old layout. The clinician share page shows the site in
the same section. Episodes are exported to FHIR as a Condition with a bounding
Encounter; the Condition does not carry the body site yet. Episodes can be linked to a visit and to documents.

The Coach sees each active condition and the six most recently recovered ones
with label, type and dates, plus the body site and side when you set them;
the note never reaches it. The MCP `get_illness_recovery` tool and the
doctor-visit summary (`healthlog://report/doctor-visit` and the
`doctor_visit_summary` prompt) carry the site and side the same way, marked
as your own text. Backups carry both fields and restore them.

## API

`GET` and `POST /api/illness/episodes`; `GET`, `PATCH` and `DELETE
/api/illness/episodes/{id}`; `POST .../restore`; `PATCH .../resolve`; `GET`
and `POST .../day-logs`; `GET .../correlation` for the per-episode
retrospective; and `GET /api/illness/insights?windowDays=` (30 to 1095 days,
with `includeRecoveryGap=true` to compute gaps for up to 24 episodes).

Since v1.39.2 an episode carries `bodySite` (text, or `null`) and `laterality`
(`LEFT`, `RIGHT`, `BOTH`, or `null` when not stated). Both are optional on
`POST` and `PATCH`; `bodySite` is trimmed and at most 200 characters. On
`PATCH` a field left out keeps its stored value, and `null` or a blank site
clears it, so a client that does not know the fields never erases them. A side
sent without a site is stored but shown nowhere in the app.
