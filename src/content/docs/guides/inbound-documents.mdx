You leave an appointment with paper: a discharge letter, a lab report, a printout
of values. Inbound documents let you upload those and have HealthLog **read out
the facts they state** — values, codes, dates — so they can join your record
without you retyping every line.

The boundary is the important part. HealthLog **transcribes what a document
says.** It does not read between the lines, draw a conclusion, or diagnose
anything from the document. It tells you what is written, and leaves what it
means to you and your clinician.

## What it does

Upload a document, and the pipeline reads it to extract the structured facts it
contains — the kind of data that fits your record:

- measured **values** (and their units),
- **codes** (lab analytes, diagnoses as written),
- **dates**.

Those extracted facts are proposed back to you. The document itself is also
stored, so you keep the original alongside whatever you accept from it.

## Review, then confirm

Nothing lands in your record automatically. The flow is **review-then-confirm**:

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="You approve each fact" icon="approve-check">
    The extracted facts are shown for you to check against the document before
    anything is saved. You confirm what is correct; you are the gate, not the
    pipeline.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Fail-closed on low confidence" icon="error">
    When the extraction is not confident enough about a value, it does not guess
    and quietly save it. It fails closed — a low-confidence read is held back for
    your attention, not written behind your back.
  </Card>
</CardGrid>

Confirmed facts flow into the existing structured stores — the same ones behind
[allergies, family history, and lab values](/guides/structured-records/) — so an
extracted reading behaves like one you keyed in yourself.

## It never interprets or diagnoses

This is the firm line. The pipeline reports the facts a document **states**; it
does not interpret them, and the Coach never diagnoses from them. A discharge
letter's wording is transcribed, not analysed into a verdict. This is the
[non-diagnostic standard](/explanation/non-diagnostic-standard/) applied to
documents: data and facts in, never a conclusion out.

## Privacy

Inbound documents reuse HealthLog's existing scan rails — the same OCR/vision
pipeline used elsewhere in the app — to read what is on the page.

- Documents are handled **for extraction**, not streamed to a model to be
  interpreted. The job is to pull out the stated facts, not to hand a letter to
  an assistant for an opinion.
- The **document store is off the AI/MCP surface.** It is not on the exposure
  allowlist and does not resolve through any read tool. The pipeline extracts
  facts for you to confirm in-app rather than exposing the documents to a model.

For the full picture of what does and does not leave your instance, see the
[privacy architecture](/explanation/privacy-architecture/).
