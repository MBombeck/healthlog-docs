The **Night timeline** card sits at the top of the sleep page
(**Insights**, **Sleep**). It draws one night's sleep stages in the order they
happened, one lane per stage the night holds, with **Awake** at the top and
**Deep** at the base, so depth increases downward. The stage totals stay in the
stacked bar below, so nothing is shown twice.

## Moving between nights

The card opens on the latest night. **Previous night** and **Next night** step
by wake day; forward stops at the latest night. The header shows the day you
woke, the time asleep, the number of awakenings, the source of the night, and
the marker that appears elsewhere when two sources disagree about it.

The axis is marked in whole hours of local time, every one, two or three hours
depending on the length of the night, also in time zones that sit half an hour
off and on the nights the clocks change. A day with no night
recorded says "No night recorded for this day."

## Nights without a timeline

Some sources report only how long you spent in each stage, not when. For such
a night the card says "This source reports stage totals without times, so there
is no timeline for this night." instead of inventing an order. A night that has
stage totals but no stage times at all says so too. The totals are still in the
stacked bar and in the night's figures.

## After travel

When the iPhone app sends the time zone a reading was taken in, a night slept
in another zone is filed under the wake day in that zone, and the timeline
draws its clock in that zone. A night slept in New York on a Berlin profile
therefore reads 23:00 to 07:00, not 05:00 to 13:00. See
[Time zones per reading](/explanation/timezone-model/#readings-taken-in-another-time-zone).

The night view behind the card is `GET /api/sleep/night`, which also carries
the `timeZone` of each session.
