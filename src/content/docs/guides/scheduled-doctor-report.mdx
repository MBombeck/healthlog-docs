Under **Settings**, **Health record**, the **Scheduled doctor report** card
files the doctor report into your [documents](/guides/document-vault/) on its
own, once a month, quarter or year has ended. It is off until you turn it on,
and it sends no notification: the PDF is simply there, in the documents and in
anything that reads them.

- **How often**: every month, every quarter or every year. The period is the
  calendar month, quarter or year in the time zone of your profile.
- **What each report covers**: the period that ended, the last 90 days, or the
  last 12 months up to its end.
- **What it contains**: what you selected for the health-record export on the
  same page. Change the selection there and the next report follows it. With
  nothing selected, nothing is filed and the card says so.

Within the hour after a period ends, the report for it is rendered on your
server and stored as a document of the kind *Doctor report*, dated the last day
of the period and titled with the period, for example `Doctor report 2026-10`.
Turning the schedule on files the period that ended most recently on the next
run. **Turn off** stops new reports; the ones already filed stay in your
documents.

## Once and only once

Each report carries the key `REPORT_SCHEDULE` / `<cadence>:<period>`, for
example `MONTHLY:2026-10`. A run that finds the key files nothing, so a retry
or a restored backup never files a period twice. If you delete a report, the
key is remembered and that period is not filed again.

## When a report is not filed

- The doctor report or the documents module is off: nothing runs, and the
  period is filed once both are on again.
- The report does not fit the document size limit or your storage quota: the
  card says so, and the next period tries again.
- Something else fails: the card says so, and the next hourly run tries again.
- An account waiting for deletion is never read.

Automatic AI reading of the document is held back, because the report is
HealthLog's own text. You can still ask for a summary from the document
itself.

## For scripts

`GET`, `PUT` and `DELETE /api/settings/report-schedule` read, set and remove
the schedule of your own record. `PUT` takes
`{ "cadence": "MONTHLY", "window": "PERIOD" }`; the cadences are `MONTHLY`,
`QUARTERLY` and `YEARLY`, the windows `PERIOD`, `LAST_90_DAYS` and
`LAST_365_DAYS`. The `GET` answer names the last period settled, how it ended,
and the period the next run files. A filed report lists in
`GET /api/documents/inbound` with `sourceSystem: "REPORT_SCHEDULE"`.
