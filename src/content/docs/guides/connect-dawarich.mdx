[Dawarich](https://dawarich.app) is a self-hosted location history. Since
v1.43.0 HealthLog can read daily movement context from it: how far you moved,
how much of that by car or by train, how many places you visited, how long
you were away from home, and whether a day was spent in another country. The
connection is part of the
[Environment module](/explanation/environmental-module/) and only runs while
that module is on.

## What is stored, and what is not

HealthLog keeps one row of numbers per day:

- total distance, distance by car, distance by train;
- the number of places visited;
- minutes away from home;
- whether the day was a travel day, and the country of the day, sealed at
  rest.

It never stores a coordinate, a track, a place name or a visit. The list of
your Dawarich places is read live when you pick your home and is not saved.
The address of the instance and the API key are stored encrypted.

## What is read

Once a night, at 03:20 Europe/Berlin, HealthLog fetches the last seven days,
ending yesterday. Connecting fetches the last 30 days, and **Fetch now** on
the card queues the last seven again. Each request carries your API key as
`Authorization: Bearer <key>`.

| Dawarich endpoint | What HealthLog keeps |
| --- | --- |
| `GET /api/v1/timeline` | total distance, distance by car (`driving`), distance by train, places visited |
| `GET /api/v1/visits` | the minutes spent at your home place, to work out the time away from home |
| `GET /api/v1/residency` | the country of each day, sealed, and whether it differs from your home country |
| `GET /api/v1/places` | nothing; the list is shown once so you can pick your home |
| `GET /api/v1/users/me` | nothing; used to check the key |

Time away from home is the length of the day minus the time a visit at your
home place covers. Without a home place, or on a day without a single visit,
it stays empty instead of reading as zero. Dawarich leaves out a mode that
moved less than half a kilometre; HealthLog reads that mode as zero for the
day.

Residency needs API access on a hosted Dawarich plan; a self-hosted instance
has it. Without it there are no travel days, and everything else works.
Dawarich keys residency by UTC date, so on a day that crosses a border late
in the evening the country can be the next morning's.

Responses are read with a size limit, and a fetch whose window ends in the
middle of a day no longer overwrites a day that was already complete.

## Connect

<Steps>

1. In Dawarich, open your account settings and copy the API key.

2. In HealthLog, turn on the Environment module, then open
   **Settings, Integrations, Dawarich**.

3. Enter the address of the instance and the key, and connect. The last 30
   days start loading.

4. Pick your home place from the list, and set your home country if you
   like (a two-letter code such as `DE`). Without one, the country you spent
   most days in over the last year counts.

</Steps>

:::caution[Run both in the same time zone]
Dawarich groups its timeline by its own time zone, HealthLog by yours. If the
two differ, a day can come back without its distances.
:::

The card follows the same layout and states as every other connection; see
[Integration status and problems](/guides/integrations/).

## An instance on your own network

A public instance works without any server setting. An instance on a private
network (a LAN address, a `.lan` name, a container on the same host) is
refused until the operator lists its exact origin:

```env
DAWARICH_PRIVATE_ORIGINS="http://dawarich.lan:3000"
```

The grammar is the one `NIGHTSCOUT_PRIVATE_ORIGINS` uses: one
`scheme://host[:port]` per entry, comma separated, no path, no wildcard and
no range. Listing a host does not open its subdomains or another port. The
unspecified address, link-local addresses and the cloud metadata range can
never be listed. Unlike Nightscout, the address you enter in the card may
carry a path, for an instance served under a sub-path behind a reverse proxy;
the grant is matched on the origin. The variable is on the compose
`environment:` whitelist; restart HealthLog after changing it. See
[Environment variables](/reference/environment-variables/#device-integrations-optional).

## Where the values show up

- The [day view](/guides/day-view/) has an **On the move** section with the
  day's figures.
- Time away from home and the distance travelled join the
  [correlations](/explanation/environmental-module/) as two more factors.
- Runs of at least two travel days that no trip covers yet are offered as
  trips under **Settings, Location & weather, Location periods**. Taking one
  fills in the dates; you pick the place. A suggestion can be dismissed.
- The Coach and the MCP `get_day` tool see the figures and whether a day was
  a travel day, never the country.

## Disconnect

**Disconnect** stops the nightly fetch and keeps the stored days.
**Disconnect and delete days** removes them as well. The days are part of the
[backup](/guides/backups/); the address and key are not, so a restored
account connects again.
