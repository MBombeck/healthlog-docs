A prescription, the visit it came from, the condition it treats and the
report that documents it are one story. Since v1.43.0 each of the six kinds of
record shows the others it belongs to in a **Related** section on its detail
page: medications, visits, lab results, documents, conditions and
vaccinations. Open a medication and you see the visit that prescribed it, the
condition it treats and the report it came from; open a visit and you see the
medications and doses given there.

The server decides what is related and sends the list with the record, so the
web app and the iOS app show the same thing.

## Which records link to which

Six kinds make fifteen pairs. Each pair is one of three things.

**A link you set.** These are stored and read from both ends:

| Pair | Where you set it |
| --- | --- |
| Lab result and visit | the lab result or the visit |
| Lab result and document | the lab result; a scan from a document sets it on its own |
| Visit and document | the visit or the document |
| Visit and condition | the visit |
| Visit and medication | the medication or the visit: prescribed, changed or reviewed there |
| Visit and vaccination | the visit or the vaccination: the dose given there |
| Document and condition | the document |
| Document and medication | the medication |
| Document and vaccination | the vaccination |
| Condition and medication | the medication: what it is taken for |

**A relation that follows from something else.** Nothing extra is stored:

- A lab result and a condition are related when both are filed against the
  same visit. The entry says **through a visit**.
- A lab result and a medication are related when the medication's efficacy
  target is that marker. The entry says **through the efficacy target**.

**No relation.** Three pairs have none, because nothing the record stores
connects them: a lab result and a vaccination (an antibody titre is a lab
result of its own, filed against the visit or the report), a condition and a
vaccination (a vaccination prevents rather than treats, and the record stores
no indication for a dose), and a medication and a vaccination (a vaccine is
kept in the immunization log, not as a medication). The visit or the
document carries the context for these.

## Set a link

The **Related** section lists what is linked and, in your own record, offers
a picker for each kind you can file from there. On a medication that is
conditions, documents and visits; on a lab result, documents and visits; on a
visit, medications and vaccinations next to the documents, lab results and
conditions it already linked. A visit links up to 100 records of each kind.

Removing a link removes only the link. Deleting either record removes its
links with it.

When you scan a lab report from a document in the vault, every result saved
from that scan is linked to the document, so the result and the report name
each other.

## Find a year or a month in a picker

The pickers open on the records around the record's own date and page to
older and newer entries. Typing searches the whole list. Since v1.43.0 a term
that names a period lists every record of that period, newest first, however
far back it sits:

- a year: `2019`;
- a month and a year, numeric, in either order: `03.2019`, `3/2019`,
  `2019-03`;
- a month name and a year in the language of the app: `March 2019`,
  `März 2019`, `Maerz 2019`, or the first three letters of a month when they
  name only one (`sep 2019`).

The sheet names the period it shows. This also works in the visit picker,
which has no text search. A term with anything else in it, such as
`LDL 2019`, stays a text search, and so does a year before 1900 or more than
a year ahead.

## What shows in a shared record

The list follows the same rules as everything else in a
[shared record](/guides/shared-access/). A kind appears only while its module
is on for the record and the grant covers its section, and a relation that
runs through a visit also needs the visit's section, because it says the
visit exists. At most 50 entries of one kind are listed.

Setting and removing links is for the record's own account. A delegate sees
the links and cannot change them.

## Backups

Every link is part of the [backup](/guides/backups/) and comes back with a
restore, including a restore into another instance.

## For API clients

The six detail reads (`GET /api/medications/{id}`, `/api/encounters/{id}`,
`/api/labs/{id}`, `/api/documents/inbound/{id}`,
`/api/illness/episodes/{id}` and `/api/vaccinations/{id}`) carry `related[]`:
`kind`, `id`, `label`, `date`, `href` and `via` (`direct`, `encounter` or
`efficacyTarget`). Links are set with `POST` and removed with `DELETE` on the
`/links` route of the record, with `{ targetKind, targetIds }` in and
`{ changed, unknown }` out. See
[the OpenAPI spec](/reference/api-endpoints/#the-openapi-spec).
