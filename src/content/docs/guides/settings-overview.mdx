Settings is split into nine groups. This page lists them in the order the
navigation shows them, says in a line what each section holds, and links to
the page that explains it. On a phone the same list opens from the section
sheet at the top of Settings.

A section that belongs to a tracking module is only listed while that module
is on, and the operator can switch a module off for the whole instance under
[Module availability](/guides/admin-overview/#module-availability). Some
actions ask you to confirm it is you (a second factor or passkey within the
last five minutes); see
[Confirming it is you](/guides/sign-in-and-security/#confirming-it-is-you).

## Account

### Account

Profile (username, display name, email, avatar, height, date of birth,
language), the identity block for the doctor report (full name, insurer,
insurance number), time zone, date and time format, metric or imperial units
and the glucose unit. The **Setup** card brings the first-run questions and the
checklist back. See [First steps](/getting-started/first-steps/#coming-back-to-it)
and [the time zone model](/explanation/timezone-model/).

### Security

Authenticator app, security keys, recovery codes, passkeys and the password,
followed by your active sessions, trusted devices and recent security
activity. See [Sign-in and security](/guides/sign-in-and-security/).

### Shared access

Who may open your record, which records you may open, managed profiles and the
log of who opened what. Only reachable while you are in your own record. See
[Give another account access](/guides/shared-access/) and
[Managed profiles](/guides/managed-profiles/).

## Tracking

### Modules

One switch per optional area (cycle, mood, sleep, glucose, labs, medications,
Coach, AI analysis, documents and more). Weight, blood pressure and pulse are
always on. A module switched off disappears from every page and keeps its data.
The module rows for medications, mood and labs open their own settings page:
injection sites, mood tags, and the biomarker catalog with on-device text
recognition for lab reports. **AI analysis** decides only whether a model
writes text for you; see
[When AI is not available](/guides/ai-providers/#when-ai-is-not-available).

## Arrangement

### Arrangement

Order and view, nothing else. One sub-page each for the dashboard, Insights,
medications, labs, the illness journal, documents and checkups; a module's
sub-page is listed while the module is on. Dashboard tiles are described in
[Dashboard customization](/features/dashboard-customization/).

## Notifications

### Notifications

Reminders that are not tied to one item: the mood reminder, the low-stock
warning for medications, Coach nudges and the checkup reminder switches. A
card names the reminders set elsewhere (doses on each medication, cycle
reminders on the Notifications page), and **Health figures in notifications**
decides whether a lock screen may show values such as the morning score. Where reminders are delivered is set
under [Connections, Integrations](#integrations). See
[Reminder channels](/guides/reminder-channels/).

Which pillars count toward the composite score. See
[Composite health score](/explanation/composite-health-score/).

### Targets

Your own target band per metric, with resets, and the glucose reference card.
A target decides which way a change counts as progress on the dashboard. See
[Blood pressure targets](/explanation/blood-pressure-targets/).

### Medical history

Conditions, allergies, family history, the emergency profile, an "about me"
note, the **Life circumstances** card, and whether this profile is shown to
the AI features. See [Record allergies and family history](/guides/structured-records/)
and [Life circumstances and AUDIT-C](/guides/life-circumstances/).

### Location & weather

Home location, travel periods, the air quality and pollen switch, and a
weather backfill. Listed while the environment module is on. See
[Environmental module](/explanation/environmental-module/).

## AI

### AI provider

Provider, model and key, the fallback chain, the response timeout, the AI
usage level and the consent card. See [AI providers](/guides/ai-providers/);
the timeout is explained under
[Response timeout](/guides/ai-providers/#response-timeout).

### Coach

The Coach switch, then its preferences (tone, reply length, thinking depth,
follow-up suggestions, excluded metrics), what the Coach remembers, and its
conversations, plans and reminders. Coach nudges moved to Notifications. See
[Coach conversations](/guides/coach-conversations/) and
[Coach plans](/guides/coach-plans/).

## Connections

### Integrations

The **Connections** list (Withings, WHOOP, Fitbit, Google Health, Apple
Health, Polar, Oura, Strava, Nightscout, Dawarich), the nutrients intake card,
the **Document archives** card for Paperless-ngx and Papra, and **Delivery
channels** (Telegram, ntfy, Web Push, webhook, email) at
`/settings/integrations#channels`. See [Integrations](/guides/integrations/)
and [Reminder channels](/guides/reminder-channels/).

### Source priority

Which source wins when two report the same metric, globally and per metric.
See [Source priority](/guides/source-priority/).

### API & tokens

Measurement, workout and document tokens, and the list of personal API tokens
with revoke. See [Push readings from your own hardware](/guides/ingest-token/)
and [Import documents](/guides/import-documents/).

### MCP connector

The state of the MCP connector, connected assistants with revoke, and the
tokens used to connect one. The switch itself is under Modules. See
[Enable the MCP connector](/guides/mcp-server/).

## Data

### Export & import

CSV exports, the full backup (encrypted `.hlx` or plain JSON), and imports
from Apple Health, Health Connect, JSON, measurement CSV, dose history, and
lab results and medications from a spreadsheet. See [Backups](/guides/backups/),
[Import Apple Health](/guides/import-apple-health/),
[Import Health Connect](/guides/import-health-connect/) and
[Import a CSV](/guides/import-csv/).

### Health record

The health-record export and, below it, clinician share links
(`/settings/sharing` opens here). See
[Clinician share links](/guides/document-vault/#clinician-share-links).

### Data & privacy

A read-only summary of encryption, what is stored, retention and the AI
posture, plus the **Workout routes** switch (keep GPS tracks of new workouts).
See [Privacy architecture](/explanation/privacy-architecture/).

### Advanced

**Delete all data** and **Delete account**, each behind a confirmation. See
[Data deletion](/account/data-deletion/).

## System

### About this app

Running version with an update check, links to the repository, changelog,
docs and credits, and **Copy support info** for a bug report. See
[Report a problem with an error id](/self-hosting/troubleshooting/#report-a-problem-with-an-error-id).
