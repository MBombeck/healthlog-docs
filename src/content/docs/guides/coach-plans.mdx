A coach plan is a durable, personal if-then intention that comes out of a Coach
conversation: a cue, an action, and optionally a target — "if it's 21:30, then
start winding down; target: lights out by 23:00." Plans give a conversation a
shape you can keep, instead of advice that scrolls away.

What else an answer shows, such as the sources the Coach read, tables and
charts of your values and follow-up suggestions, is on
[Coach conversations](/guides/coach-conversations/).

## Proposed, never self-activated

When you name a goal or agree to one of its suggestions, the Coach can write a
plan as a **proposal**. Since v1.41.0 the proposal sits under the answer that
offers it, with two choices:

- **Take on this plan**: the plan becomes active and starts counting. The
  Coach replies that it will check in with you after the plan's review window,
  which is 7 to 56 days.
- **Not now**: nothing is kept.

Only your tap activates a plan. The Coach cannot activate, edit, or resurrect
one on its own, and declined proposals never feed back into what the Coach
knows about you. Accepted plans do: an active plan is part of what the Coach
starts every question with, together with one sentence on how it is going,
which the server works out from your own readings (the latest week against
the two weeks before the plan began). The daily briefing mentions up to two
active plans the same way.

A proposal is one if-then plan tied to one metric. Its wording goes through
the same check as an answer, so a plan that would change a dose or a
medication is never proposed. It is only kept when the answer that shows it is
stored, so an answer that fails leaves no plan behind. There is at most one
proposal per answer and at most three open at a time; one you leave unanswered
lapses after 14 days. Proposals need a provider that can call tools; see
[Coach conversations](/guides/coach-conversations/#plans).

## The plans page

**Coach → Plans** lists everything in three groups:

- **Proposed** — waiting for your accept/decline.
- **Standing** — active plans, plus any that have reached their review date.
- **Past** — met, ended, or reviewed plans.

From there you can mark a plan met, end it, or remove it. A plan created with
a review date comes back for review when the date arrives — the pattern is a
small self-experiment: agree on a change, run it for a few weeks, then look at
what happened.

## Reaching your whole conversation history

The Coach conversation list pages through your **entire** history — earlier chats
are no longer stranded once you pass the most recent handful. **Search** runs on
the server, matching a conversation by its title, so an old thread is reachable
by name rather than only by scrolling. A document conversation stays tagged with
its document badge in the same list (see [the document
vault](/guides/document-vault/#chatting-about-a-document)).

## Privacy

The cue, action, and target of every plan are encrypted at rest, like the rest
of your sensitive content. Plans live on your instance and go nowhere else.
