The emergency card is the emergency information from your health profile, in a
form you can carry: on your phone, in your wallet, and behind a QR code a first
responder can scan. It holds what you have entered and nothing more.

## What is on it

The card is put together from what you already keep in HealthLog:

- blood type, implants and devices, emergency contacts, the advance directive,
  the organ donor declaration and the note, from **Settings > Health profile >
  Emergency information**;
- your active allergies, severe ones first, with the reaction where recorded;
- ongoing chronic conditions and the conditions you wrote in your health
  profile;
- medications that are active, not paused and not ended;
- a pregnancy or the weeks after a birth, when
  [cycle tracking holds that state](/guides/pregnancy/);
- your date of birth.

Open **Emergency card** from the account menu: the avatar menu at the foot of
the sidebar, or the avatar in the top bar on a phone. It no longer has a place
of its own in the sidebar or the **More** sheet. You also reach it from the
button on **Emergency information** in **Settings > Medical history**, the card
in **Settings > Health record**, or by searching for "emergency card". The
address is `/emergency-card`.

It is the card of the record on screen. In your own record that is yours. The
guardian of a managed profile holds the profile's card, so inside that profile
the page shows and manages the profile's card. Someone who shares an adult's
record never sees or manages that person's card, at any access level.

## On your phone, without a connection

HealthLog does not work offline on purpose: a signed-in page is never stored on
the device, so a lost phone does not hand out your record. **Save offline copy**
downloads the card as one HTML file instead. It has no script and makes no
network request, so it opens from the Files app or a home-screen bookmark with no
connection. It shows what the card held on the day you saved it, and says so.
Save it again after a change.

## In your wallet

**Wallet card (PDF)** prints both sides of a credit-card-sized card on one A4
page: print at 100 percent, cut along the outline, fold at the dashed line. The
front holds blood type, allergies, conditions, medications and a pregnancy; the
back holds contacts, implants and the declarations. Text that does not fit is
shortened, and the card says the full card is behind the QR code.

## The QR link

The QR link lets someone read the card without signing in. You decide:

- **What it shows.** Tick each item; nothing you leave unticked is ever read for
  the link. The form starts with blood type, allergies, conditions,
  medications, pregnancy, implants and contacts ticked. The date of birth, the
  note and the two declarations are shown only when you tick them.
- **When it ends.** Never, or in 30 days, a year or two years.
- **Whether it lives.** Revoke it at any time, and the code answers nothing from
  then on.

The link and its code are shown once, when you make the link, so the page offers
**Wallet card with QR code (PDF)** right then. Making a link asks you to confirm
it is you. The page tells you how often the link was opened and when last, and
every opening is written to your audit log. Only one link is live at a time; to
change what it shows, revoke it, make a new one, and print the card again.

### What can stop the link

- **Changing your password**, or an administrator resetting it, revokes the
  link.
- **A restore.** The link is not part of a backup. After a restore, make a new
  link and print the card again.
- **The API switch.** If the operator turns off **API** ("Allow API endpoints
  and API tokens.") under **Admin > Push and channels**, the link stops
  working, and so do the [cycle](/guides/cycle-calendar-feed/) and
  [care](/guides/care-calendar-feed/) calendar feed links. An operator who
  wants the emergency links to keep working leaves that switch on.

The link is a credential like a [clinician share link](/guides/document-vault/#clinician-share-links).
HealthLog stores only its hash, rate-limits every attempt before looking it up,
answers every unknown, revoked or expired link with the same "not found", keeps
it out of its own logs, and never caches the page. Repeated openings cannot lock
the card for the person who needs it.

## Wallet passes

An Apple Wallet pass would need a pass-type certificate from Apple's developer
program, signed on the server for every pass. HealthLog does not ship one, so it
is not offered; the printed card and the iOS app's lock-screen options cover the
same need.
