When you close open registration, new users join by **invite link**. Operators
mint expiring links from a dedicated admin section and keep a redemption history
of who joined when.

## Close registration

Registration is on by default (the bundled image ships it enabled). The toggle
lives in `app_settings.singleton.registrationEnabled`; flip it from the admin
panel. With registration closed, the public sign-up form is gone and the only
path to an account is a valid invite.

## Mint an invite

In the admin **Invites** section, create a link. Each invite carries:

- a **status** (active / used / expired / revoked),
- a **uses** count and an optional cap,
- an **expiry**,
- a **redemption history** of who redeemed it and when.

The link and its **QR code are shown exactly once at creation** — copy them
then. The QR encodes the same redemption URL, handy for onboarding a household
member from their phone.

The API surface is `GET` / `POST /api/admin/invites` and
`GET` / `DELETE /api/admin/invites/{id}` (cookie-only admin — Bearer tokens
cannot reach it).

## Revoke

Revoking an invite disables the link and QR immediately but **keeps the
redemption history**, so you retain the audit trail of who joined through it.
Already-created accounts are unaffected — revoking an invite does not remove a
user who already redeemed it.

## Redeem

A new user opens the invite URL (or scans the QR), lands on a scoped
registration form, and creates their account. The first-user-becomes-admin rule
only applies to the very first account on the instance; invited users are always
regular users.
