You want the app to remind you of a recurring check (a blood panel, a skin
check, a blood-pressure reading), to keep your doctors in one place, and to
record visits, past and upcoming, including procedures and surgeries. All of
this lives under `/checkups`, which has four tabs: **Checkups** (preventive
care), **Visits**, **Procedures** and **Body sites**. None of it is a module.
Preventive-care reminders can target core vitals and free-text exams that
belong to no module, so they are available to every account and cannot
be switched off, by users or by the admin. The only module influence is on
the link pickers: linking a lab result, a document or an illness episode to
a visit follows the module that owns it.

## Preventive-care reminders

Add a reminder with a label, what it targets, an interval, a first due date,
and the hour to notify (09:00 by default). Targets come in three kinds:

- **A vital**: weight, systolic blood pressure, pulse, blood glucose, blood
  oxygen, body temperature.
- **A body-composition value**: body fat, fat mass, fat-free mass, muscle
  mass, lean body mass, bone mass, total body water, visceral fat, BMI, waist
  circumference.
- **A mental-wellbeing screening**: PHQ-9, GAD-7, WHO-5, SCI. These default
  to every 28 days.
- **Free text** with no target: a blood panel, a dermatology check. These are
  satisfied only by marking them done.

Intervals are presets of 7, 14, 28, 30, 90, 180 or 365 days, a custom "every N
days", or an RFC 5545 rule such as `FREQ=YEARLY`. An optional location field
records where the check happens.

Each reminder is a card sorted by its next due date, which the server
computes; the card stays neutral whatever the state, and due-ness reads as
"today", "tomorrow", "in N days" or "overdue since N days" in calendar days
of your profile timezone. The action depends on the target: a free-text exam
is simply marked done; a measurement-linked reminder opens the measurement
form, so completing a blood-pressure reminder records an actual reading. You
can also snooze or skip (a skip does not count as done), and each reminder
keeps a history. A dashboard card shows the next few due.

Reminders notify through the ordinary channels (APNs, Telegram, ntfy, Web
Push, webhook) as the measurement-reminder type, which is on by default; see
[Reminder channels](/guides/reminder-channels/). Booster reminders from the
[vaccination record](/guides/vaccinations/) are reminders of this kind too.

## Practitioners

`/checkups/practitioners` is your own list of doctors and practices. Each has
a name (a person or a practice, whichever you think of first), a free-text
specialty, practice, address, phone, and an encrypted note (parking, which
entrance, who to ask for). The list is scoped to your account so one
household member's cardiologist never appears in another's picker. It is
deliberately not in Settings: a list of your doctors is record content, not
configuration.

## Visits

A visit records a date and time, a status (planned, done, cancelled,
no-show), a kind (routine, acute, specialist, preventive, emergency,
hospital, therapy, procedure or surgery, other), a practitioner, an
encrypted reason and an encrypted outcome. A visit can also carry a body site
in your own words (encrypted) and a side: left, right or both. Future dates are allowed up to ten years ahead; that is
what makes a visit an appointment. Cancelled and no-show visits keep their
row so the history stays honest. The list shows upcoming visits soonest-first
and past visits newest-first.

A visit can link up to 100 documents, lab results and illness episodes each,
and a vaccination dose can point at the visit it was given at. When a
document's date is within seven days of a visit, the app suggests the link.
Filing a visit from a due checkup marks that checkup satisfied.

### Procedures and surgeries

Since v1.39.1 you record a procedure as a visit of the kind **Procedure or
surgery**. The form then offers a body site ("knee", "gallbladder") and a side.
A visit of another kind shows the two fields only once it has a site or a
side, so switching the kind never hides what you typed. While you type, the
site field suggests the sites already in your record, from visits and from
conditions; you can still type anything. Only the date is required, as for
any visit. A visit you entered earlier, for example as a hospital stay, can be
switched to this kind by editing it; its date, reason, outcome, practice and
links stay as they are.

The **Procedures** tab lists every procedure that took place, newest first.
Planned, cancelled and no-show procedures are not in it; a booked operation
appears under upcoming visits. Above the list, each body site you have used is
offered as a filter with its count, and a search field matches every word you
type against the body site, the side and the reason. "left knee" finds a
procedure with the site "Knee" and the side left, and a procedure recorded
with only a reason ("knee arthroscopy") is found by "knee" as well.

The body site is encrypted at rest, so the search runs on the server: it
decrypts your own procedures and matches there. Nothing about the site is
stored in plain text for the search.

### Body sites

A body site can sit on a visit and, since v1.39.2, on a condition in the
[illness journal](/guides/illness/#body-site). The **Body sites** tab brings
both together: what was done on your left knee, and what is wrong with it.

The tab lists every body site in your record as a chip with the number of
visits and conditions that carry it, most used first. Spellings that differ
only in case, accents or spacing count as one site ("Knee", "knee",
"Knée"); the chip shows the spelling of the latest-dated visit with that site,
or of the latest condition when no visit has it. Different words stay
different sites: "knee" and "left knee" are two chips.

Tap a chip to see what is filed there, and tap it again to clear the choice.
The match is on the whole site, so "knee" does not pick up "kneecap". When at
least one record at the site states a side, a second row offers **All sides**
and a chip for each side that occurs:

- **Left** and **Right** also show what is recorded on **Both sides**,
  because a condition in both knees is in the left knee too.
- **Both sides** shows only records on both sides.
- A record whose side is **Not stated** shows only under **All sides**. It
  could be either side, and listing it under one would claim what nobody
  wrote.

The selection has two lists:

- **Procedures and visits**: every visit with that site, of any kind and any
  status, newest first. Each shows what was done (the reason, else the
  practitioner, else the kind), the date, the kind, the site with its side,
  and its linked documents, lab results and conditions.
- **Conditions**: every condition with that site, newest onset first. Each
  shows its label (which opens the condition), "Since" the onset or the onset
  to the recovery date, its type, the site with its side, and its linked
  documents and visits. The condition's note is not shown here.

A linked document opens in the vault and a linked condition opens its page;
lab results and visits are listed with their date but without a link, a visit
by its kind. Conditions, and the sites that only conditions carry, appear only
while the illness module is on for the record. A condition's page links here
with its site and side already picked. With no site anywhere in the record,
the tab says "No body site recorded yet".

### Appointment reminders

A planned visit automatically gets one reminder that fires on the day of the
appointment at the appointment's own hour, through the same channels as any
other reminder. There is no lead time to configure. It is a one-shot: with
no cadence it can never fire twice. Rescheduling re-anchors the same
reminder; cancelling or marking no-show disables it without deleting it. The
notification carries no done or later buttons, because the appointment is
managed through the visit itself. These reminders are kept out of the
checkup list, the daily digest and the preventive-care insight so the list
does not fill with appointments.

### Report for a visit

With a planned visit within the next 14 days, the health-record export panel
offers to prepare a report for that appointment. Pressing it only sets the
end of the custom date range; nothing changes until you press it, and you
can dismiss it.

## Where it goes

Visits are a selectable leaf of the doctor report, not in the standard
template. **Surgical history** is a leaf of its own and part of the standard
template: every procedure that took place, oldest first, with its date, what
was done (the visit's reason), the body site with its side and the outcome.
Like the vaccination record it covers the whole record, not only the report
period. A report selection saved before v1.39.1 keeps what you chose and does
not include it until you tick it. It also appears on the clinician share page
when the link includes it. The FHIR export carries no visits or procedures
section yet. Through MCP, upcoming appointments are a field on the
preventive-care tool and past visits have their own tool, which can be
narrowed to procedures; without a time window it then reads the whole record.
Backups carry the body site and the side and restore them.

The **Body sites** tab reads in the Health background section. Someone you
share your record with needs access that includes it for the tab to load;
without it the site field on a condition offers no suggestions, and a
condition's site is shown as plain text instead of a link. Conditions appear
in the tab only when their access also includes Illness. A link into a section
they cannot read keeps its line but loses its name and date: it reads "A
document not shared with you", "A lab result not shared with you" or "A
condition not shared with you".

## API

Practitioners: `GET` (with `q` search) and `POST /api/practitioners`; `GET`,
`PATCH`, `DELETE /api/practitioners/{id}`; `POST .../restore`. Visits: `GET`
and `POST /api/encounters`; `GET`, `PATCH`, `DELETE /api/encounters/{id}`;
`POST .../restore`; `POST` and `DELETE .../links`; `GET
/api/encounters/suggest`; `GET /api/encounters/procedures` (with `q` and
`laterality`), which answers `{ procedures, bodySites, total }`. A visit
carries `bodySite` and `laterality` (`LEFT`, `RIGHT`, `BOTH` or null) on every
kind; the procedure kind is `PROCEDURE`.

Body sites: `GET /api/body-sites` answers `{ sites }`, one entry per site with
`bodySite`, `procedures` (the count of visits with the site, of any kind),
`conditions` and `sides` (`{ laterality, count }`, `null` for not stated),
most records first. With `site` it adds `selection`: `bodySite`, `laterality`,
`visits` (each a visit with its `links`) and `conditions` (each with `links`
to `documents` and `visits`). `site` names a whole site, compared without case,
accents or extra spaces, and is 1 to 200 characters; `laterality` (`LEFT`,
`RIGHT` or `BOTH`) narrows it and needs `site`. An empty or overlong site, an
unknown side, or a side without a site answers `422` with
`meta.errorCode: body-site.invalid`. `conditions` is `null`, not `[]`,
when the caller may not read Illness or the record has the illness module
off. A link the caller may not read comes back with `redacted: true` and no
`label` or `date`. The read covers the newest 500 visits and 500 conditions
that carry a site. The full shapes are in the
[OpenAPI document](https://github.com/MBombeck/HealthLog/blob/main/docs/api/openapi.yaml).
Reminders: `GET` and `POST
/api/measurement-reminders`; `GET`, `PATCH`, `DELETE
/api/measurement-reminders/{id}`; `POST .../complete`, `.../satisfy`,
`.../skip`, `.../snooze`; `GET .../history`.
