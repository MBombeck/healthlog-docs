## What this site covers

HealthLog is a self-hosted personal-health-tracking app — a PWA plus a native
iOS client — that brings weight, blood pressure, pulse, glucose, body
composition, sleep, mood, menstrual cycle, and medication tracking onto one
timeline you own. It runs from a single `docker compose up` on a NAS, homelab,
or small VPS, syncs from the devices you already own, and keeps everything
encrypted at rest.

These docs are organised the way the [Diátaxis](https://diataxis.fr/) framework
suggests: a learning-oriented tutorial to get you running, task-oriented
how-to guides for specific jobs, factual reference material, and explanation
pages that cover the reasoning behind the design.
