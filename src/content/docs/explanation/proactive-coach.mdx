Most apps that reach out to you are trying to pull you back in. The proactive
Coach is built to do the opposite: to say one warm, useful thing in the morning
when there is genuinely something to say, and otherwise to stay quiet.

It is **a gentle check-in, not an alert.** No red badges, no urgency, no streak
guilt. A calm morning note phrased as an invitation — and, often, nothing at
all.

## What triggers it

The nudge watches a small set of signals — the same kinds of trends the
[health scores](/explanation/health-scores/) and insights already surface, such
as recovery picking up or blood pressure settling. When one of them has moved in
a way worth a sentence, the nudge has something to open with. When nothing has
genuinely changed, it has nothing to say, and so it says nothing.

## The discipline behind it

Generation is a **scheduled morning job**, and that timing is deliberate:

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="Scheduled, not reactive" icon="information">
    The nudge is composed by a morning job — never warmed on a page visit, never
    generated when a screen mounts. Opening the app does not trigger an outreach;
    the only thing that does is the morning schedule.
  </Card>
  <Card title="A morning frame" icon="sun">
    Running early makes a "good morning" framing safe and keeps the note to one
    calm moment in the day rather than an interruption whenever you happen to
    look.
  </Card>
</CardGrid>

This matches a standing rule across HealthLog: generation happens on a schedule
or by an explicit button, never on mount, so a provider is never saturated by
people simply browsing.

## Cadence and anti-nag

The nudge is governed by suppression rules whose whole purpose is to make it rare
enough to be welcome:

- **At most one a day.** Never more.
- **Never two days running on the same focus.** It will not repeat the same theme
  morning after morning.
- **Suppressed when there is nothing genuine to say.** No manufactured nudge to
  hit a cadence. Silence is the default, not the failure case.
- **Suppressed when you already engaged the Coach recently.** If you have talked
  to the Coach in roughly the last day, it holds back — you do not need an
  unprompted check-in on top of a conversation you just had.

There is no streak to keep, no guilt for a quiet stretch, no notification sent
just to be seen.

## How it reads

The message is a **wellness prompt phrased as an invitation, not an imperative**.
It is localized to your language, opens with a greeting, and offers — never
orders — to take a closer look together. Something closer to "if you like, I can
show you what's behind it" than "you need to check this."

:::caution[It never quotes your own notes back]
The nudge references the **focus category** — the kind of thing it noticed — not
your private words. It does not interpolate your decrypted self-context sentences
back at you verbatim. You will see "your recovery has been picking up," not your
own note read aloud. This is a deliberate guard, not an incidental one.
:::

And, like everything else the Coach says, it **describes, never diagnoses**. It
points at a trend and offers a conversation; it does not tell you what a trend
means for your health. That boundary is the
[non-diagnostic standard](/explanation/non-diagnostic-standard/).

## Where it arrives

The nudge is delivered over the same channels as your other notifications — push,
Telegram, and the rest — in your own language. To choose where it lands, see
[reminder channels](/guides/reminder-channels/).
