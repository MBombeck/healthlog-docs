HealthLog is a self-hosted personal health tracker that runs from a single
`docker compose up`. It covers the metrics most people actually log — weight,
blood pressure, pulse, body composition, blood glucose, sleep, mood, menstrual
cycle, and medication compliance — and brings them together in one dashboard
with clinical reference ranges, overridable with the targets your clinician set.

Try the [live demo](https://demo.healthlog.dev) to see what a working install
looks like, or jump straight to
[Self-host in 10 minutes](/getting-started/self-host-in-10-minutes/).

## What it does

- **Every vital on one timeline.** Weight, blood pressure, pulse, glucose,
  body composition, sleep, SpO₂, mood, and cycle — charted with trends, moving
  averages, and reference ranges from established guidelines.
- **Your devices, one server.** Withings and WHOOP sync over OAuth2, Google
  Health/Fitbit connects experimentally, an Apple Health `export.zip` folds your
  full history in, and the native iOS app streams HealthKit live. A per-metric
  [source priority](/guides/source-priority/) decides which reading is canonical
  when wearables overlap.
- **Medication tracking that tells the truth.** Flexible schedules (weekly
  injections, weekday-only, intervals, PRN, cyclic), a configurable intake
  window per dose, and a traceable dose history where every slot reads taken,
  late, skipped, or missed — the same ledger the
  [compliance rate](/explanation/medication-compliance/) is computed from.
- **AI insights you own.** A daily briefing, health scores, correlations, and a
  conversational coach grounded in your own measurements. Bring your own OpenAI
  or Anthropic key, or point at a local endpoint so nothing leaves your network.
- **Clinician-ready output.** A doctor-report PDF generated client-side, a
  read-only [HL7 FHIR R4 API](/reference/fhir-api/), and scoped, time-limited
  share links you revoke after the visit.
- **Private by construction.** AES-256-GCM
  [encryption at rest](/explanation/encryption-at-rest/) with zero-downtime key
  rotation, passkey login, server-side sessions, strict CSP, and no third-party
  tracking anywhere.

## Who it is for

HealthLog is built for people who want their health data on their own server —
a NAS, a homelab, or a small VPS — and who do not want to hand it to a vendor
cloud to read a seven-day weight trend. It does not ship a sensor and does not
try to be a ring or a strap; instead it aggregates the readings your devices
already collect, plus the ones they do not hold (cuff blood pressure,
finger-stick or CGM glucose, medication intake), and keeps them on hardware you
control. See [how it compares](/features/#how-it-compares).

## Status

Active development — new releases land roughly weekly. Behaviour, API shapes,
and database schema can change between minor versions; migrations are
forward-only. If you self-host, pin a tag, take a backup before every upgrade,
and skim the [changelog](https://github.com/MBombeck/HealthLog/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md)
before pulling `latest`. The current line is v1.16.
