The dashboard opens with a **Today** view: the day's read at the top, the
familiar tiles and charts unchanged below it. It is the surface that turns
HealthLog from a place you record your health into one that meets you each day.

Everything in Today reads an insight **prepared overnight** — nothing is
generated when you open the app, so it loads instantly and never triggers a
provider call on a page visit. What follows is what each piece is and where its
number comes from.

## What Today shows

- **A health score.** One ring with the day's
  [Health Score](/explanation/composite-health-score/), computed from your own
  measurements and graded pillar by pillar against published reference bands.
  Below full breadth it names how many areas of health it was built from, and
  it refuses only when nothing in your record is readable yet.
- **The day's lead read.** The single most relevant line from the briefing
  prepared overnight — what changed, or what's worth your attention today.
- **A "worth a look" rail.** A short list of priority items, each a single tap
  to the right place: a dose window that's open, an integration to reconnect, a
  check-up coming due, a fresh ECG, a milestone reached.
- **A coach check-in.** When a coach plan is due for a look-back, it surfaces
  here as a calm card (see below).

The greeting stays; the tiles and charts sit just below, unchanged. A layout
saved with the older opt-in hero card simply ignores that setting — the two
never stack.

## Morning freshness

The score and read are prepared overnight, but last night's sleep usually
hasn't synced by the time the digest is built. So Today shows a **provisional**
score first, and refreshes the sleep-dependent parts on their own the moment
last night's sleep arrives — from Withings, WHOOP, Apple Health, Google Health,
Fitbit, Oura, or Polar. By the time you look, the day is up to date.

Until sleep is in, Today **says so plainly** rather than showing a stale or
empty score. Each skipped refresh is recorded, so a morning read that stayed
provisional can be traced to its cause.

## The worth-a-look rail

Each item on the rail is a single tap to the place it points at. An item that
only needs your awareness — a milestone, a new ECG, an elevated-at-rest note —
can be **dismissed** once you've seen it; the section tightens when the last one
is gone. Items that need an action (an open dose window, an integration to
reconnect) stay until you act on them.

Since v1.30.31 a dose appears on the rail **from the moment it is takeable**, not
only once it is late — until then Today could report nothing needing attention
while a dose was due. The two faces read differently: a due dose is a calm,
informational note, an overdue one keeps the warning, and a day with everything
taken still shows the all-clear. Push reminders are unchanged — they still fire
only once a dose is late.

## The coach check-in

When a coach plan reaches its review date, it comes back as a calm card in Today
with three choices — **keep**, **adjust**, or **let go** — one tap each. Letting
go retires the plan quietly; silence never changes it. This is the loop a coach
plan is built for: agree on a change, run it for a few weeks, then look at what
happened. See [Coach plans](/guides/coach-plans/) for how a plan is created and
reviewed.

## Quiet milestones

Reaching a steady stretch inside your target range, or a new personal best, is
marked with a calm moment on the rail. Milestones are **states you've reached,
not streaks to keep** — so there's nothing to break and nothing to nag. A
missed day is invisible; there is no "you broke it" to reach.

Three kinds surface:

- **A personal best** — a fresh record for a core vital.
- **A return to range** — a metric that has settled back inside your own usual
  range. Only the calm arrival is narrated; leaving the range is never marked.
- **A sustained stretch** — one durable state per week held inside your range,
  up to four weeks. The set is closed; there is no ever-climbing counter.

A milestone surfaces **once**, on the day it was reached, and never resurfaces —
its reach-date is a settled historical fact. At most one appears per day, the
most meaningful first.

:::note[Milestones are not the achievements page]
These daily milestones are distinct from the gamification
[achievements](/features/achievements-hidden/) surface, which keeps its own
streak, milestone, and consistency badges. Today's milestones are durable states
reached — a separate, calmer primitive.
:::

## The optional daily note

A once-a-day morning notification carrying the day's read is available, **off by
default** and opt-in per channel under Notifications. It carries the refreshed
digest once sleep is in, never repeats within a day, and is a calm note — never
an alert. It rides the same [reminder channels](/guides/reminder-channels/) as
the rest of the app.

## Dashboard tiles

Today also brings tiles for readings that were synced and charted but never had
a place on the home strip: heart-rate variability, blood oxygen, breathing rate,
wrist temperature, and — for anyone with a body-composition scale — muscle mass,
body water, and bone mass. Each tile appears only once you have that reading,
and every one can be toggled or reordered under **Settings → Dashboard**.
