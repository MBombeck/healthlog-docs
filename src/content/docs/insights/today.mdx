The dashboard opens with a **Today** view: the day's read at the top, the
familiar tiles and charts unchanged below it. It is the surface that turns
HealthLog from a place you record your health into one that meets you each day.

Everything in Today reads an insight **prepared overnight** — nothing is
generated when you open the app, so it loads instantly and never triggers a
provider call on a page visit. What follows is what each piece is and where its
number comes from.

## What Today shows

- **A health score.** One ring with the day's
  [Health Score](/explanation/composite-health-score/), computed from your own
  measurements and graded pillar by pillar against published reference bands.
  Below full breadth it names how many areas of health it was built from, and
  it refuses only when nothing in your record is readable yet.
- **The day's lead line.** One sentence about the day with something in it,
  never a greeting. With an AI provider it comes from the briefing prepared
  overnight; without one it is built from your own readings. See
  [The Today overview](#the-today-overview).
- **A short "Today" list** (since v1.40.2). What is on today, in a few lines:
  medications taken so far, an appointment, last night, your vitals, the cycle
  day, Rest Mode.
- **A "worth a look" rail.** A short list of priority items, each a single tap
  to the right place: a dose window that's open, an integration to reconnect, a
  check-up that is due or overdue, a visit booked for today or the next two
  days, a fresh ECG, a milestone reached.
- **A coach check-in.** When a coach plan is due for a look-back, it surfaces
  here as a calm card (see below).

The tiles and charts sit just below, unchanged. A layout
saved with the older opt-in hero card simply ignores that setting — the two
never stack.

## The Today overview

Since v1.40.2 the top of the card says what matters today instead of greeting
you. It has three parts: a lead line, a short list headed **Today**, and the
score ring.

### The lead line

The first line is a sentence with content. HealthLog picks it in this order:

1. A short reaction to something that landed today, written by the AI model
   (only with a provider and while AI analysis is on).
2. The first sentence of the overnight briefing that says something. A
   greeting such as "Good morning." and a sentence that only repeats the score
   in the ring are skipped.
3. Without either, a sentence built from your own readings, about the day's
   strongest signal:
   - a vital outside your personal range, the one furthest out first, for
     example "Resting heart rate is at 64 bpm, above your usual range of 52 to
     60 bpm." When more vitals are outside, the sentence says how many;
   - a night at least an hour longer or shorter than your usual;
   - all your recent vitals inside their usual range;
   - an ordinary night ("Last night you slept 7 h 20 min, close to your
     usual.").

When nothing holds, there is no lead line; the card never fills the space with
a filler sentence. Everything in point 3 comes from the server and works on an
instance without any AI provider.

### The Today list

Below the lead line, a list headed **Today** shows up to five facts about the
day, always in this order:

| Fact | Shows | When |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Rest mode** | **Day 3** | An illness episode is open and the Illness module is on |
| **Medications** | **2 of 3 taken** | Something is scheduled today and the Medications module is on. On the day the clocks go back, the whole 25-hour day counts |
| **Appointment** | **Today 14:30** or **Tomorrow 09:00**, with the practitioner or the kind of visit | A visit booked for today or tomorrow, nothing further out. Several on the same day read "+1". Left out when the worth-a-look rail already names that appointment |
| **Last night** | **7 h 20 min, 40 min less than usual** | Last night's sleep is in and the Sleep module is on. Within 20 minutes of your usual it reads "close to your usual" |
| **Vitals** | **4 checked, all in your range**, **Resting heart rate above your range**, or **2 of 4 outside your range** | Recent readings of your vitals, against your personal ranges |
| **Cycle** | **Follicular, day 9** | The Cycle module is on and knows the day |

Three rules hold for every line:

- **A fact is a statement.** No line asks you to do anything. The worth-a-look
  rail below is the only place on the card that does.
- **A switched-off module has no fact.** The server leaves it out, so no client
  can show it.
- **Nothing is said twice.** When the lead line is built from a fact, such as
  last night's sleep, that fact leaves the list.

The list is never padded: a quiet day has fewer lines, and no lines is a valid
answer. A phone shows the first four, a wide screen all five. Each line opens
the page it belongs to.

### The ring and "steady for"

The health score ring stays. Under it, instead of a change since yesterday, the
card can say **Steady for 3 weeks**: the score has stayed in the same band and
within two points of today's value, computed the same way, for at least two
whole weeks. A gap of more than three days without a stored score ends the
run. The read looks back 120 days; a run longer than that reads **Steady for at
least 17 weeks**. When the score has not held for two weeks, nothing is said.

## Morning freshness

The score and read are prepared overnight, but last night's sleep usually
hasn't synced by the time the digest is built. So Today shows a **provisional**
score first, and refreshes the sleep-dependent parts on their own the moment
last night's sleep arrives — from Withings, WHOOP, Apple Health, Google Health,
Fitbit, Oura, or Polar. By the time you look, the day is up to date.

Until sleep is in, Today **says so plainly** rather than showing a stale or
empty score. Each skipped refresh is recorded, so a morning read that stayed
provisional can be traced to its cause.

## The worth-a-look rail

Each item on the rail is a single tap to the place it points at. An item that
only needs your awareness — a milestone, a new ECG, an elevated-at-rest note —
can be **dismissed** once you've seen it; the section tightens when the last one
is gone. Items that need an action (an open dose window, an integration to
reconnect) stay until you act on them.

The rail shows at most three items. Two kinds keep their place even when more
compete for it; they still appear in their usual order, below an open dose:

- **Check-up due** names up to three reminders from `/checkups` and counts the
  rest ("+2"). A reminder that repeats less often than once a week shows from
  the morning of its due day, stays while it is overdue until it is done,
  skipped or snoozed, and always keeps the item a place (see
  [When a reminder is due](/guides/checkups-and-practitioners/#when-a-reminder-is-due)).
  A weekly or more frequent one, such as a weigh-in, shows only once its time
  has come and gets no guaranteed place.
- **Appointment coming up** for today names every visit booked for the day in
  one item, and stays until the day ends, also after a visit has started.

The next day with a booked visit within the coming 48 hours gets its own item
when there is room, worded "tomorrow" or "the day after tomorrow" by the
calendar of your profile timezone.

Which kinds may appear is chosen under **Settings → Appearance → Dashboard**,
**Today highlights**. A choice saved before appointments were one of the kinds
hid them without anyone switching them off. The upgrade to v1.39.2 switches
**Appointments** on once for every such choice; switching it off again sticks.

Since v1.30.31 a dose appears on the rail **from the moment it is takeable**, not
only once it is late — until then Today could report nothing needing attention
while a dose was due. The two faces read differently: a due dose is a calm,
informational note, an overdue one keeps the warning, and a day with everything
taken still shows the all-clear. Push reminders are unchanged — they still fire
only once a dose is late.

## The coach check-in

When a coach plan reaches its review date, it comes back as a calm card in Today
with three choices — **keep**, **adjust**, or **let go** — one tap each. Letting
go retires the plan quietly; silence never changes it. This is the loop a coach
plan is built for: agree on a change, run it for a few weeks, then look at what
happened. See [Coach plans](/guides/coach-plans/) for how a plan is created and
reviewed.

## Quiet milestones

Reaching a steady stretch inside your target range, or a new personal best, is
marked with a calm moment on the rail. Milestones are **states you've reached,
not streaks to keep** — so there's nothing to break and nothing to nag. A
missed day is invisible; there is no "you broke it" to reach.

Three kinds surface:

- **A personal best** — a fresh record for a core vital.
- **A return to range** — a metric that has settled back inside your own usual
  range. Only the calm arrival is narrated; leaving the range is never marked.
- **A sustained stretch** — one durable state per week held inside your range,
  up to four weeks. The set is closed; there is no ever-climbing counter.

A milestone surfaces **once**, on the day it was reached, and never resurfaces —
its reach-date is a settled historical fact. At most one appears per day, the
most meaningful first.

:::note[Milestones are not the achievements page]
These daily milestones are distinct from the gamification
[achievements](/features/achievements-hidden/) surface, which keeps its own
streak, milestone, and consistency badges. Today's milestones are durable states
reached — a separate, calmer primitive.
:::

## The optional daily note

A once-a-day morning notification carrying the day's read is available, **off by
default** and opt-in per channel under Notifications. It carries the refreshed
digest once sleep is in, never repeats within a day, and is a calm note — never
an alert. Since v1.40.2 its text carries no number: no reading, dose or score
appears on a locked phone. The figures stay in the app's own lead line. It rides the same [reminder channels](/guides/reminder-channels/) as
the rest of the app.

## Dashboard tiles

Today also brings tiles for readings that were synced and charted but never had
a place on the home strip: heart-rate variability, blood oxygen, breathing rate,
wrist temperature, and — for anyone with a body-composition scale — muscle mass,
body water, and bone mass. Each tile appears only once you have that reading,
and every one can be toggled or reordered under **Settings → Appearance → Dashboard**.
