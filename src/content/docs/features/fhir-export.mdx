HealthLog speaks [HL7 FHIR R4](https://hl7.org/fhir/R4/), the wire format clinical systems use to exchange health records. You meet it in three places. This page says what each one is for and where you reach it in the app; the endpoint table, the resource list, the scope rules, and the module gate are on the reference page, [FHIR read API](/reference/fhir-api/).

## The health-record export

Open **Settings → Health record**, pick a date range and exactly which metrics and record types to include, and download one of three files: a clinical PDF for a doctor handover, a FHIR R4 document Bundle (`application/fhir+json`) for a system that reads FHIR, or a zip package with both and a short README. The PDF and the Bundle read the same aggregator, so they carry identical numbers. If you fill in your name, insurer, and insurance number under **Settings → Account**, they appear on the PDF cover and in the Bundle's `Patient` resource.

<img src={exportFlow.src} alt="One export request with a format, a date range, and a leaf-level selection list reads the shared aggregator once and produces two artefacts: an enriched PDF for a doctor handover, and an HL7 FHIR R4 document Bundle holding a Patient resource, LOINC-coded Observations with a blood-pressure panel, MedicationStatements, and a DiagnosticReport that ties the Observations together" />

The export is [scoped by selection](/reference/fhir-api/#health-record-export): what you did not tick is not in the file. It honours the doctor-report module switch under **Settings → Modules**; with the module off, the export and the FHIR API both answer 403 rather than the record, and nothing is deleted.

## The read-only FHIR API

A clinical system, or a script of your own, can pull your record as FHIR resources from `GET /api/fhir/*` with a Bearer token minted under **Settings → API & Tokens**. It is read-only; nothing can be written through it. Start at `/api/fhir/metadata`, which describes what this server supports. The full list is under [Endpoints](/reference/fhir-api/#endpoints).

## The clinician share link

For a person with no account and no software, the **Sharing** card under **Settings → Health record** mints a time-limited, passphrase-protected link to a read-only view of a frozen selection of your record. The same link offers the PDF and the FHIR Bundle of that selection for download, and you revoke it when the visit is over. A share link never opens the FHIR API. See [Sharing documents with a clinician](/guides/document-vault/#sharing-documents-with-a-clinician).

## Another account on your instance gets none of these

A [shared-access grant](/guides/shared-access/) lets somebody with their own account read or maintain your record inside the app, but a delegate cannot export it, reach the FHIR API, or mint a share link at any grant level. Taking a copy of a record out is a different act from keeping the record; the reasoning is in [What a shared record is](/explanation/record-sharing/).

:::caution[Read-only and not a clinical record system]
The FHIR API and the export describe your tracked data in a standard shape. They are for sharing with a clinician, not for use as a system of record or for any automated clinical decision.
:::
